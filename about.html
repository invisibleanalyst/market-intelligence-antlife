Antlife is an investment intelligence practice. We help founders and investment teams examine the markets, costs, and assumptions behind a capital decision.

How we work with you.

You bring the commercial question and the context. Together we agree on the options to compare, the evidence already available, and what needs investigation.

We research the market, test the economics, and give you a recommendation you can examine. You can follow its sources, see where an assumption matters, and decide what to test next.

For a founder considering recovered textiles, that meant defining a feedstock to pilot. For an independent expansion study, it meant revisiting the cost of serving a customer.
