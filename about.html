About Antlife / Investment Intelligence

Antlife brings market research and commercial analysis to decisions involving capital.

FROM INFORMATION

The work is to

We investigate questions about markets, sectors and commercial opportunities. The aim is a view you can examine: what the evidence supports, what remains an assumption, and where further work is needed.

The selected work includes commissioned desk research into textile recycling in Kenya and an independent model exploring East African expansion economics.

Our scope is agreed for each engagement. Research and commercial analysis do not replace legal, tax, audit or engineering expertise.
