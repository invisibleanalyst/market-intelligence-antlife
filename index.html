Commercial feasibility · Kenya

Could used clothing become yarn?

A founder saw a possible second life for Kenya’s used clothing. Before investing in machinery, they needed to know which fibres to buy and what it would cost to recover them.

Follow the study ↓

Supply

Which fibres are worth recovering?

Used clothing arrives as a mixture of materials. The desk study identified polyester as a candidate for testing. A founder still needs to know how much usable fibre a typical batch will yield.

Process

What survives sorting?

A bale price tells only part of the story. Sorting time, contamination and recovery losses determine the cost of usable input. Comparing suppliers on that basis can change the sourcing decision.

Pilot

What should the first trial prove?

The recommendation was to trial a small mechanical process with a defined feedstock. A pilot would measure recovery yield and test output quality before the founder commits to plant equipment.
