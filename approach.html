How we reach a recommendation.

Every engagement begins with a choice. We agree on the question, examine the evidence, then test what could change the answer.

Frame

What decision are you facing?

We agree the question, the intended use of the work and the boundaries of the enquiry.

Investigate

What does the evidence support?

We examine relevant public and client-provided information, recording sources and gaps.

Test

What has to be true?

We compare options, test commercial assumptions and explore the conditions that could change the answer.

Explain

What follows from the analysis?

We explain the recommendation, the basis for it, and the next piece of evidence worth collecting.

What supports the answer?

Each recommendation identifies its sources, assumptions, and judgement.

Published evidence

External information with a traceable source, date and stated scope.

Client information

Inputs supplied for an engagement, identified separately from independent research.

Modelled assumptions

Inputs and calculations used to explore possibilities, rather than report events.

Analytical judgement

Our interpretation of the available evidence, with its limitations made explicit.

Define the question with us.

A short description of the decision is enough to start.
